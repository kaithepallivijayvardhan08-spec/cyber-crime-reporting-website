Frequently Asked Questions

Is my information secure?

Yes, we use 256-bit encryption and follow strict data protection protocols to ensure your information remains confidential.

How long does resolution take?

Resolution time varies by case complexity. Simple cases may be resolved in 7-10 days, while complex investigations may take longer.

Can I report anonymously?

Yes, we offer anonymous reporting options for those who wish to remain confidential.

What types of crimes can I report?

You can report all types of cyber crimes including phishing, identity theft, online fraud, cyber bullying, and more.
