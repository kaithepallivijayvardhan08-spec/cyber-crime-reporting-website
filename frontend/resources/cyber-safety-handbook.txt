CYBERSAFE - CYBER SAFETY HANDBOOK
Educational quick guide | CyberSafe academic prototype

1. Protect your accounts
- Use a different, long password for each important account.
- Turn on two-step verification where it is offered.
- Never share a password, PIN, or one-time password with anyone.
- Do not approve a login or payment request you did not start.

2. Check messages and links
- Look closely at the sender, web address, spelling, and unexpected urgency.
- Do not open unexpected attachments or sign in through a message link.
- Contact the organisation using its official app, website, or phone number.
- A padlock or familiar logo alone does not prove a message is genuine.

3. Pay safely
- Use the official bank or payment app and check the payee before confirming.
- A UPI PIN is used to send money; do not enter it to receive money.
- Never scan a QR code or approve a collect request just because someone asks.
- Turn on transaction alerts and contact your bank quickly about an unknown debit.

4. Keep devices safer
- Install operating-system and app updates from official sources.
- Use a screen lock and back up important files.
- Avoid sensitive transactions on public Wi-Fi; use your mobile network if possible.
- Install apps only from trusted stores and review app permissions.

5. Protect personal information
- Share less personal and location information publicly.
- Review account privacy and recovery settings regularly.
- Be careful with friend requests, impersonation, and requests for private photos.

If you think a cybercrime has happened, save relevant messages, URLs, transaction details, and dates. Do not edit the original evidence. For incidents in India, use the official National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal: https://www.cybercrime.gov.in/ . For financial cyber fraud, report promptly through the official helpline 1930 and contact your bank/payment provider.

This student project guide is general awareness information, not legal or investigative advice.
